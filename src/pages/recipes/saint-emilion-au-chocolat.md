Serves 6. 25 minutes, plus chilling overnight.

## Ingredients

- 225 g dark chocolate, chopped
- 200 ml milk
- 55 g softened butter
- 100 g sugar
- 1 egg yolk
- Pinch of salt
- 100–120 g cantuccini (almond biscuits)
- 50 ml dark rum
- 20–30 ml milk, for the rum
- Lightly whipped cream, to serve

## How to

1. Beat the butter and sugar until creamy, then mix in the egg yolk.
2. Heat the milk until steaming and pour it over the chocolate. Wait 2 minutes, then stir until smooth.
3. Let it cool slightly, then gradually mix it into the butter mixture. Add the salt.
4. Break the biscuits into chunks and put ⅔ of them in the serving dishes.
5. Mix the rum with the extra milk and drizzle it over the biscuits.
6. Pour the chocolate mixture over the biscuits and top with the rest of the biscuits.
7. Chill overnight. Serve with lightly whipped cream.

## Notes

- For kids, make separate portions with milk instead of rum.
- The egg yolk isn't cooked, so use a pasteurized one.
