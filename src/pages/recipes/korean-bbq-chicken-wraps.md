## Ingredients

- 1600 g boneless skinless chicken thighs, excess fat trimmed
- 2 tsp salt
- 3 tsp black pepper
- 3 tsp garlic powder
- 4 tsp onion powder
- 100 g gochujang paste (mild or hot)
- 50–60 g minced garlic
- 35 g sesame seeds
- 60 g honey
- 70 g light soy sauce

## How to

1. Trim excess fat from the chicken thighs, add the seasonings and the marinade ingredients, mix, and set aside.
2. Spread the marinated chicken evenly on a lined large sheet pan and add cooking spray.
3. Bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes, until golden.
4. Serve in wraps with [the carrot, cucumber and red onion salad](/recipes/carrot-cucumber-red-onion-salad).
