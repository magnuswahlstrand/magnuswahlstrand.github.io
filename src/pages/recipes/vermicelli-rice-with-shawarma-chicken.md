## Ingredients

### Vermicelli rice

- 3 dl basmati rice
- 1 dl roasted vermicelli
- ~6 dl chicken stock or water
- 1–2 tbsp butter
- Salt

### Chicken

- Chicken thighs
- Cumin
- Coriander
- Paprika
- Garlic
- Lemon
- Olive oil
- Cinnamon (optional)

### On the side

- Yoghurt + lemon + garlic
- Tomato and cucumber salad

## How to

### Vermicelli rice

1. Wash the rice.
2. Fry the vermicelli briefly in butter.
3. Add the rice, then the stock, and salt.
4. Cook covered for ~12–15 minutes.
5. Let rest for 10 minutes, then fluff.

### Chicken

1. Marinate the thighs in cumin, coriander, paprika, garlic, lemon and olive oil.
2. Roast or grill hard.

### Sides

1. Stir lemon juice and grated garlic into the yoghurt, and salt it.
2. Make the tomato and cucumber salad.

## Notes

- Turkish-style bulgur pilaf with vermicelli: replace the rice with coarse
  bulgur and add tomato paste.
