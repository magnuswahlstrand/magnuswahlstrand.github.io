A quick weekday breakfast. The egg mash is just boiled eggs, mayo and crispy
chili oil — incredibly good.

## Ingredients

- 2 boiled eggs
- A dollop of mayonnaise
- A splash of crispy chili oil
- Sourdough bread
- Avocado
- Sesame seeds

## How to

1. Mash the eggs together with the mayonnaise and crispy chili oil.
2. Toast the sourdough bread.
3. Top the toast with avocado, then the egg mash, and finish with some sesame seeds.
