This isn't exactly the traditional Bang bang ji si from Sichuan — there's no
sesame paste in it — but I didn't find a better name.

## Ingredients

- 5–6 chicken thighs (with or without skin)
- ~9 slices of ginger
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 3 scallions
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 red chili pepper
- 1 tbsp crispy chili
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp oyster sauce
- 3–4 tbsp neutral oil

## How to

1. Cut the scallions and separate the whites from the greens.
2. Sous vide the chicken thighs at 68°C for 2 hours or so. I put 2–3 fillets per bag, plus 3 slices of ginger and the scallion whites.
3. Take the thighs out of the bags and slice or tear them into the pieces you want. Save some of the juices from the bags for later.
4. Crush the garlic, grate 1 tbsp of ginger, slice the chili, and mix it all with the scallion greens in a small bowl.
5. Heat the oil until the first sign of smoke, then pour it over the ingredients from the previous step.
6. Mix in the crispy chili, soy sauce and oyster sauce.
7. Add liquid from the sous vide bag to taste.
8. Pour the sauce over the chicken and serve.

## Notes

- You can also boil the thighs instead of sous viding, but be careful not to overcook them.
