Lebanese garlic sauce.

Makes one bowl, about 4 servings.

## Ingredients

- 2 egg whites
- 3 garlic cloves
- 2–3 dl rapeseed oil
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

## How to

1. Crush the garlic and blend it with the egg whites, lemon juice and salt until white and foamy.
2. Keep blending while adding the oil in a thin, slow stream, until thick and fluffy. Go slowly, or it will split.
3. Chill for an hour before serving.

## Notes

- Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge.
