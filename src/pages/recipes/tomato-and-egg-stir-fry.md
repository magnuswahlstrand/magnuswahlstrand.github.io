## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 1 can of cherry tomatoes
- 1 scallion
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp white pepper
- Sesame oil
- 1½ tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 3 tbsp neutral vegetable oil
- 2 tsp sugar (or more)

## How to

1. Beat the eggs with some of the salt, the white pepper, sesame oil and Shaoxing wine.
2. Chop the scallion.
3. Heat about 2 tbsp oil over high heat. Add the eggs and scramble very briefly until just set but still soft, stirring with a wooden spoon. Remove.
4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil, then the tomatoes. Stir-fry for a few minutes, then add the scallion, the rest of the salt and the sugar.
5. Return the eggs and gently fold them through the tomatoes for ~30 seconds.

## Notes

- The original recipe adds 50 ml water with the tomatoes — skipped here.
- Serve with steamed rice.
