Serves 5. Serve with rice or mashed potatoes.

## Ingredients

- 1–1.2 kg pork shoulder, cut into 3–4 cm chunks
- 2 yellow onions, roughly chopped
- 2–3 garlic cloves
- 2 tart apples
- 300 ml apple cider
- 200 ml water
- 1–2 tbsp chicken stock concentrate
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 200 ml heavy cream
- 1½–2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- Salt and black pepper
- Butter or oil

## How to

1. Season the pork with salt and pepper. Brown well in batches, then set aside.
2. Sauté the onions for a few minutes. Add the garlic and thyme.
3. Pour in the cider and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom.
4. Add the water, stock concentrate, and 1 tbsp Dijon. Return the pork to the pot.
5. Cook at high pressure for 35 minutes, followed by 10–15 minutes natural pressure release.
6. Add the cream, apples cut into wedges, and the remaining Dijon.
7. Simmer uncovered for 10–15 minutes, until the sauce has reduced to your liking.
8. Season with apple cider vinegar, salt, and plenty of black pepper.
