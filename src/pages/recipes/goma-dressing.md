## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2–3 tsp sugar or honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1–2 tbsp water, to thin
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted and crushed (optional)

## How to

1. Mix everything except the water until smooth.
2. Gradually add water until it's easily pourable.
3. If using, fold in toasted and crushed sesame seeds for a more authentic flavour.
