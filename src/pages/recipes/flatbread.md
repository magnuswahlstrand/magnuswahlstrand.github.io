Makes 6.

## Ingredients

- 300 g plain flour, plus extra for dusting
- ½ tsp salt
- 50 g butter
- 185 ml milk

## How to

1. Melt the butter in the milk.
2. Mix with the flour and salt, then knead for a couple of minutes until smooth.
3. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
4. Divide into 6 pieces and roll out thin, about 20 cm across.
5. Cook in a hot, dry pan for 1–1½ minutes per side, until puffed with golden spots.
6. Keep the cooked breads wrapped in a tea towel to stay soft.
