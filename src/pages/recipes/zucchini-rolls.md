## Ingredients

- 2 zucchini (long, straight)
- 2–3 tbsp tapenade
- 100 g cream cheese or chèvre
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Lemon zest
- Black pepper
- 1 garlic clove
- Toothpicks

## How to

1. Mix cream cheese, tapenade, lemon zest, garlic, pepper, and a few drops of olive oil. Let rest if you have time.
2. Cut zucchini into long thin slices (~2–3 mm).
3. Cook ribbons in a hot oiled pan, 30–60 sec per side until soft. Salt lightly, cool on paper towel.
4. Lay each ribbon flat, spread a thin layer of filling, roll tight, and secure with a toothpick.
