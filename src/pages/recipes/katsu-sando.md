## Ingredients

### Tonkatsu sauce

- 100 ml ketchup
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp Japanese soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated

### Cabbage salad

- 150 g cabbage
- ½ tbsp Japanese soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar or white wine vinegar

## How to

1. Mix ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, honey and garlic for the tonkatsu sauce.
2. Finely shred the cabbage.
3. Toss the cabbage with soy sauce, sesame oil and vinegar.
