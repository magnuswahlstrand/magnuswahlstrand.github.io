Serves 4–5. About 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 800 g–1 kg chicken thigh steaks, skin on
- Salt, pepper and neutral oil

### Korean BBQ sauce

- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey or brown sugar
- 1–1½ tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp water

### Gochujang mayo

- 100 ml mayonnaise
- 1–2 tsp gochujang
- 1 tsp lime juice or rice vinegar
- ½ tsp sesame oil
- ½ tsp honey
- Water, to thin

### To serve

- Tortilla wraps
- Sliced cucumber
- Spring onions
- Sesame seeds
- [Carrot, cucumber and red onion salad](/recipes/carrot-cucumber-red-onion-salad)

## How to

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Fry skin-side down over medium heat for 8–10 minutes, then flip and cook 5–8 minutes more, until 74°C inside.
2. Simmer the BBQ sauce ingredients for 2–4 minutes, until glossy and slightly thickened.
3. Mix the gochujang mayo, adding water until it can be drizzled.
4. Rest and slice the chicken. Warm the tortillas and fill with cucumber, chicken, BBQ sauce, mayo, spring onions, sesame seeds and carrot salad.

## Notes

- Keep the BBQ sauce separate until serving so the chicken skin stays crispy.
