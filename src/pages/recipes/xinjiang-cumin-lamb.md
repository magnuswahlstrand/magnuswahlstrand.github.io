## Ingredients

- 700 g lamb stew chunks
- 2–3 tsp cumin seeds, coarsely crushed
- 1–2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 yellow onion
- 1–2 tbsp soy sauce
- Salt
- ~1 dl water or broth
- Coriander, to finish
- Spring onion, to finish

## How to

1. Sear the lamb hard in a hot pot, season lightly with salt.
2. Sauté onion and garlic with cumin seeds, ground cumin, and chili flakes.
3. Add soy sauce and about 1 dl water/broth.
4. Pressure cook 25–30 min on high, then 10 min natural pressure release.
5. Take out the meat and stir-fry it hard with extra cumin seeds and chili until dry and roasted.
6. Finish with coriander and spring onion.

## Notes

- Lamb stew chunks work great here — better than finer cuts for flavour and tender meat.
- A pressure cooker is perfect since stew chunks can be tough.
