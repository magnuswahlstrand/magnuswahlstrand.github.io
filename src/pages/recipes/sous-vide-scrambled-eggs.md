## Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 4 tsp whole milk
- 4 tsp heavy cream
- 25 g butter

## How to

1. Heat sous vide to 75°C.
2. Brown the butter in a pan until dark brown. Set aside.
3. Whisk eggs, milk, and cream well, then mix in the brown butter. _If ingredients are cold, wait until warm enough not to chill the butter too much._
4. Vacuum seal and cook for 16 minutes. Take the bag out 2–3 times and squash a bit to get an even cook.
5. Eat.
