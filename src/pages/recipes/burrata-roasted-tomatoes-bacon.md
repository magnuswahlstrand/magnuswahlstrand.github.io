About 25 minutes. Works as a starter or a light dinner.

## Ingredients

- 2 burrata (125 g each)
- 500 g cherry tomatoes
- 3 garlic cloves, whole and crushed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and black pepper
- 150 g bacon
- Fresh basil
- Sourdough bread, to serve

### Hot balsamic honey

- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp chili oil

## How to

1. Roast the tomatoes and garlic with olive oil, thyme, salt and pepper at 220°C for 15–20 minutes, until the tomatoes burst.
2. Fry the bacon crispy in a dry pan and break it into pieces.
3. Mix the honey, balsamic vinegar and chili oil.
4. Put the burrata on the tomatoes, scatter over the bacon and drizzle with the hot balsamic honey.
5. Top with basil and serve with toasted bread.
