## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
- ½ cucumber, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- Feta cheese
- Parsley or mint
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon
- ½ tsp cumin
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Sumac or chili flakes (optional)

## How to

1. Mix the dressing: olive oil, lemon, cumin, salt, and black pepper.
2. Toss the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion with the dressing.
3. Add feta cheese and parsley or mint.
4. Top with sumac or a little chili flakes if you have it.
