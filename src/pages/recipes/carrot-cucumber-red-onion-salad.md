## Ingredients

- 300 g cucumber, thinly sliced
- 200 g carrots, grated
- 200 g red onion, thinly sliced
- 20 g sesame seeds
- 40 g rice vinegar
- 30 g sriracha

## How to

1. Thinly slice the cucumber and red onion, and grate the carrots.
2. Mix the rice vinegar and sriracha into a dressing.
3. Toss the vegetables with the dressing.
4. Top with sesame seeds.
