On the value of spending 15 minutes or less per week to write a summary of the
main things accomplished during the week, current blockers, and key tasks for
next week. The text should be short enough for someone to read in 5 minutes. The
goal is to provide insight of your work to your direct manager (or your team, or
other stakeholders) and build trust.

**Reflection:** I like the 15-5 format, and have done similar things in the past.
Usually as a newsletter for my team. At my current job we have a system of DAILY
reports, I think that is a bit much, and it feels more like a chore rather than a
help.
